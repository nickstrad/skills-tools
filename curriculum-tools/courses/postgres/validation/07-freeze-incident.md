incident. No thresholds were lowered, large XID batch burned, control/catalog state falsified or
forced anti-wraparound worker observed. The fixture has one known coordinator and one final
decision; it does not establish consensus or multi-host availability. Absence of a GID alone does
not prove a particular business outcome, which is why the effect and full ledger are independently
checked after restart. Cleanup is part of accepted execution, not deferred maintenance.
