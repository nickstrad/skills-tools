## Source takeaways

The official page describes foundational Kubernetes and cloud-native knowledge and a multiple-choice
exam. Its topic areas include Kubernetes, orchestration, application delivery, and cloud-native
architecture. This identifies the credential; it does not verify an individual's results or prove
advanced operational expertise. [Official KCNA description](https://training.linuxfoundation.org/certification/kubernetes-cloud-native-associate/).

## Connections and possible local experiments

Our course-selection implications:

- **Kubernetes:** assume common vocabulary and routine usage. Prefer selected deep investigations
  of controller behavior, resource conflicts, scheduling, termination, networking, and recovery.
- **Docker/container runtime:** use familiar commands to expose unfamiliar kernel behavior. Avoid
  a mandatory tour of container CRUD or another introduction to images and basic deployment.
- **Networking and observability:** recap familiar concepts briefly; add experiments only when
  packet evidence, failure behavior, or measurement changes the learner's understanding.

## Limits and open questions

Do not confuse KCNA with CKA or CKAD, or infer kernel/database/consensus mastery from its name.
The learner's own production experience and explanation of a mechanism are additional evidence.
Review individual gaps without making the learner repeat an entire introductory course.
